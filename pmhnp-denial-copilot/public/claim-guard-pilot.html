PMHNP Claim Guard Pilot

This pilot handles one narrow job: take Tebra exports or remit-style claim files from a PMHNP practice, flag likely denials before submission, organize denied claims after they land, route the next action, learn from reviewer feedback, and prove ROI during the pilot.

Open client app Start onboarding intake

What this pilot is for

This pilot is designed to support PMHNP claim protection and denial recovery in a practical, office-friendly way:

  • Turn Tebra exports, denial lists, and remit-style CSV/JSON files into pre-submit risk queues and post-denial recovery worklists
  • Flag likely auth, telehealth, documentation, eligibility, timely filing, or enrollment failures before submit and route the right next action after denial
  • Track reviewer-confirmed outcomes, confidence shifts, label drift, dollars recovered/protected, denials prevented, and staff time saved

Trust and safety: This is a pilot support tool. Human review is required before decisions or claim actions. It does not replace professional billing judgment, payer policy review, or compliance processes.

1) Discovery and fit

We start with a brief conversation about workflow, payer mix, pre-submit leak points, denial pain points, and the exports your office can provide.

2) Guided setup

Setup stays simple: upload exports first, then optionally move into human-gated read-only sync later.

3) Pilot worklist period

Your team works from a claim-risk and denial-recovery queue that learns from reviewer-confirmed outcomes instead of staying a static rules demo.

4) Review and next steps

At the end of pilot, we review recovery impact, preventable denial patterns, pre-submit catch rate, drift in labels, and whether the workflow deserves expansion.

Current limits

  • No guarantee of automatic claim approval or reimbursement outcomes
  • No self-serve payer or Tebra writeback in this repo
  • No fabricated external data proof: ROI still depends on real pilot inputs and tracked outcomes

Questions are welcome. If you want to see whether this pilot fits your office, start with the Client App intake flow and we can review your exports together.
