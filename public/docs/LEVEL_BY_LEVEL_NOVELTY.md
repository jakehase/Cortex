# Level-by-level novelty

This is not a full description of every level. It is a map of where the more distinctive implementation ideas live.

## L2
Browser/discovery layer tied to research routing.

## L7 / L22
Memory and retrieval layers, especially where they support contextualization and durable recall.

## L9
Architecture/complexity-oriented chain activation and higher-order design routing.

## L13 / L29 / L32 / L34
Creativity-governor path with idea generation, reframing, synthesis, and validation.

## L15
Deliberation / council role in multi-perspective reasoning paths.

## L24
Nexus/orchestration role: route selection, chain forcing, and coordination of multi-level behavior.

## L34
Validation role as an explicit architectural stage rather than a hidden afterthought.

## Cross-level novelty
A lot of Cortex novelty is cross-level rather than level-local.

Examples:
- forced research chain
- forced creativity chain
- coding/architecture chains
- fallback and validation chains
- capability-aware control-plane behavior

## Key point

Do not think of novelty as “which single level is coolest.”
Think of it as:
- what each level contributes
- how levels are composed
- what routing/control-plane behavior exists around them
